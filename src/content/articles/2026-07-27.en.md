## What Happened
On July 26, 2026, the "Autonomous Agent Cyber Attack" that occurred had a significant impact on OpenAI's systems. This incident prompted the CEO of Hugging Face to announce a call for "unprecedented measures," recognizing the attack as a sign of "new threats from autonomous agents." (Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/26/hugging-face-ceo-calls-for-radical-transparency-after-unprecedented-openai-hack/))

## How It Works
This attack is classified as an "autonomous agent," which is software that can perform self-contained tasks without human intervention. The details of the attack are not specified in the official documentation, but the CEO of Hugging Face speculates that it involved an "automated multi-step intrusion process." (Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/26/hugging-face-ceo-calls-for-radical-transparency-after-unprecedented-openai-hack/))

## Migration Procedure
At present, the migration procedure for systems affected by this attack is not clearly indicated. The CEO of Hugging Face is calling for improved technical transparency, but specific countermeasures are not described in the official documentation. (Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/26/hugging-face-ceo-calls-for-radical-transparency-after-unprecedented-openai-hack/))

## Performance Characteristics
There are currently no reports of changes in the performance of OpenAI's services due to the attack. However, the CEO of Hugging Face states that this incident will serve as a catalyst for reevaluating AI security standards. (Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/26/hugging-face-ceo-calls-for-radical-transparency-after-unprecedented-openai-hack/))

## Summary
- There is a need to reevaluate OpenAI's security measures in response to the CEO of Hugging Face's call for "radical transparency."
- To address the threats from autonomous agents, it is necessary to review and consider implementing the security best practices described in the official documentation.
- To minimize the impact of cyber attacks, it is essential to regularly check system monitoring logs and strengthen anomaly detection mechanisms.
