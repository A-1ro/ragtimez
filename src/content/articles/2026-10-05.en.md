## Background of Google's Open-Source Bug Bounty Program Freeze
In October 2026, Google froze its open-source bug bounty program. The decision is attributed to the rapid increase in AI-generated submissions, which overwhelmed the program's operations. From a technical perspective, it is speculated that the low quality and inaccuracy of AI-generated content created a situation where it was difficult to distinguish between legitimate bug reports and AI-generated ones, leading to a significant increase in maintenance costs. (Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/04/google-froze-its-open-source-bug-bounty-program-due-to-a-significant-rise-in-ai-submissions/))

## Challenges Posed by the Increase in AI Submissions
The rise in AI-generated bug reports is imposing new burdens on security teams. Especially when AI-generated reports trigger false positives, human verification work increases. This phenomenon is a factor that reduces the efficiency of bug bounty programs. As a technical countermeasure, enhancing AI-generated report filtering algorithms and re-examining the process of human secondary verification are being considered. (Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/04/google-froze-its-open-source-bug-bounty-program-due-to-a-significant-rise-in-ai-submissions/))

## Impact on Open-Source Security
This freeze has the potential to affect the entire open-source community. Especially in small to medium-sized projects, the halt of bug bounty programs may lead to a regression in security measures. On the other hand, building a framework to properly manage new risks associated with the evolution of AI technology is urgent. Technicians need to explore best practices for security verification through collaboration between AI and humans. (Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/04/google-froze-its-open-source-bug-bounty-program-due-to-a-significant-rise-in-ai-submissions/))

## Summary
- The freeze of Google's open-source bug bounty program has led to a re-recognition of the importance of the reliability of AI-generated reports and filtering technology.
- Security teams need to work on optimizing the bug detection process through collaboration between AI and humans.
- Open-source projects should consider designing new security measures that take into account the impact of AI.
- Technicians should promote the establishment of quality evaluation standards for AI-generated content and practical verification of its utilization.
- Efforts are required to utilize official documentation and technical blogs to clarify the boundary between AI and security.
