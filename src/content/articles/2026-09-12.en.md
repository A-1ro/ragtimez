## What Has Changed
Y Combinator's Garry Tan is recommending that US open-weight AI labs utilize the "distill" technology for front-end models. This move aims to strengthen US open-weight options that are not dependent on China, with the goal of optimizing and decentralizing AI models. "Distill" refers to the technology of transcribing large front-end models (high-precision but high computational cost models) into more efficient smaller models.
