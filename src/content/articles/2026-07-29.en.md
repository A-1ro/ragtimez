## Allegations of MCP Gateway Theft by Runlayer

According to a report by TechCrunch, Runlayer, an MCP startup, has filed a lawsuit against Rippling, a provider of payroll and HR management services. The core of the lawsuit is that Rippling evaluated Runlayer's MCP Gateway product and then chose to build a similar product in-house. This sequence of events, where Rippling "evaluated and then switched to in-house development," is the central claim made by Runlayer, serving as the basis for the lawsuit alleging theft of product ideas.
