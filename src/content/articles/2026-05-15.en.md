## ChatGPT Pro's Personal Asset Management Feature Released in the US

OpenAI launched a preview of the personal asset management feature for ChatGPT Pro users in the US on May 15, 2026. This feature allows users to securely connect their financial institution accounts and view spending visualization dashboards.

It supports over 12,000 financial institutions and is available on both the web and iOS versions of ChatGPT. According to OpenAI, over 200 million people per month are already using ChatGPT for budgeting and investment advice, and with the improved reasoning capabilities of GPT-5.5, it can now handle more complex context-dependent financial questions.

Users can ask specific questions like "Plan to buy a house in Chicago within 5 years" or "Analyze this month's travel expenses." However, it is noted that this feature is not a substitute for professional financial advice.

(Source: [A new personal finance experience in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/personal-finance-chatgpt))

## Financial Data Integration and Privacy Protection Mechanisms

The core of this feature is combining the user's actual financial context with GPT-5.5's reasoning capabilities. Based on the data from connected financial accounts, ChatGPT can identify spending patterns, understand trade-offs, and support planning for important decisions.

Regarding privacy and security, OpenAI emphasizes a design that allows users to maintain control over their data. While specific security implementation details are not disclosed in the official announcement, it is stated that strict protection measures are applied to the handling of financial data.

Through collaboration with ecosystem partners like Intuit, it will also be possible to bridge the gap from answers to actual actions. This is expected to support not just the provision of information, but the execution of actual financial management tasks.

(Source: [A new personal finance experience in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/personal-finance-chatgpt))

## Phased Rollout and Quality Evaluation Efforts

OpenAI is starting with a small-scale preview, learning from actual use to improve the experience, and carefully expanding it. Feedback is being collected from testers in the ChatGPT Pro community, and the accuracy and quality of the feature are being evaluated.

This phased approach considers the characteristics of the feature, which handles highly confidential financial data. By understanding real-world usage patterns and identifying potential issues early, the goal is to ensure quality when expanding more widely.

Currently, only Pro users in the US are targeted, but expansion to other regions or pricing plans may be considered in the future. A specific expansion timeline is not provided in the official announcement.

(Source: [A new personal finance experience in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/personal-finance-chatgpt))

## Summary

- Using ChatGPT Pro's personal asset management feature, users can connect accounts from over 12,000 financial institutions and achieve real-time spending analysis and future planning.
- By combining GPT-5.5's reasoning capabilities with actual financial data, it becomes possible to devise specific, context-dependent financial strategies, such as "a 5-year plan to buy a house in Chicago."
- Through partnerships with companies like Intuit, analysis results from ChatGPT can be directly linked to actual financial management actions, integrating the workflow from information gathering to execution.
