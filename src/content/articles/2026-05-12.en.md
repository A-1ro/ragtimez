## Amazon Finance Achieves 92% Acceleration in Regulatory Compliance Tasks

The Amazon Finance team has significantly streamlined its response to regulatory inquiries by leveraging generative AI on AWS. Regulatory analysis tasks that previously required manual effort and took several days to several weeks to complete have been reduced by 92% in time, thanks to an AI solution centered around Amazon Bedrock.

This initiative is gaining attention as a pioneering example of generative AI adoption in the financial services industry. The Amazon Finance Technology team is expanding AI applications beyond regulatory compliance to contract reviews and financial forecasting, while maintaining enterprise-level security and compliance.

(Source: [Delivering Regulatory Insights 92% Faster Using AWS with Amazon](https://aws.amazon.com/solutions/case-studies/amazon-finance-case-study/))

## Team-Specific Knowledge Base for Information Management

Amazon Finance's generative AI solution adopts a system where each team builds and maintains its own dedicated knowledge base. This knowledge base stores team-specific documents and reference materials, enhancing the accuracy of responses to regulatory inquiries.

The system is configured around Amazon Bedrock and collaborates with other AWS services to realize scalable AI applications. Since each team's knowledge base is managed independently, departmental information security is maintained while enabling the generation of specialized responses tailored to each team's area of expertise.

This architecture allows for the immediate reference of relevant past cases and internal guidelines, enabling the generation of accurate responses to complex regulatory inquiries.

(Source: [How Amazon Finance streamlines regulatory inquiries by using generative AI on AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-amazon-finance-streamlines-regulatory-inquiries-by-using-generative-ai-on-aws/))

## Broad Application of Generative AI Across the Financial Industry

The Amazon Finance case demonstrates the wide range of possibilities for generative AI adoption in the financial services industry. Generative AI is being utilized in various areas, including fraud detection, contract review, financial forecasting, and personalization, with tangible outcomes in each field.

AWS AI provides financial institutions with enterprise-grade security and compliance features to strengthen fraud prevention and automate operations. This enables innovation to be accelerated while meeting stringent regulatory requirements.

For financial institutions, it is crucial to improve efficiency while maintaining regulatory compliance and security. AWS's generative AI solutions provide the foundation for achieving this balance.

(Source: [AWS for Financial Services - Generative AI](https://aws.amazon.com/financial-services/generative-ai/))

## Summary

* Combining Amazon Bedrock with dedicated knowledge bases accelerates regulatory compliance tasks by 92% for each department, reducing analysis time from several weeks to just a few hours.
* Building team-specific knowledge bases enables the construction of high-precision regulatory inquiry response systems that leverage department-specific documents and reference materials within the company environment.
* Introducing AWS generative AI solutions in areas such as fraud detection, contract review, and financial forecasting can achieve comprehensive automation of financial operations while maintaining enterprise-level security.
