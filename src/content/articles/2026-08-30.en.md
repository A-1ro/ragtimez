## AI's Impact on Healthcare  
AI is said to be transforming biology from "discovery" to "engineering," and the utilization of open datasets is expected to accelerate the transformation of the medical field. This movement enables a new approach that can replace traditional research methods.  

(Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/29/were-not-doing-30-bets-a-year-vijay-pande-on-betting-small-after-running-4-billion-at-a16z/)）  

## The Importance of Open Datasets  
Vijay Pande emphasizes that open and shared data can accelerate AI applications in medicine more than closed datasets. This can promote collaboration among researchers and potentially improve the training accuracy and generalization ability of models.  

(Source: Ibid [techcrunch.com]）

## Challenges in Clinical Trials and AI's Potential  
The high cost of clinical trials remains a significant challenge, and AI is being noticed as a means to optimize that process. For example, automating patient selection and data analysis can be expected to increase the efficiency of trials.  

(Source: Ibid [techcrunch.com]）

## Summary  
- Utilizing open datasets can build a collaborative framework for AI-driven medical research  
- The key to AI applications in the medical field is reducing costs and increasing efficiency in clinical trials  
- The movement of AI-native venture capital is expected to accelerate technological innovation in the medical field
