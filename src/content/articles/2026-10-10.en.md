## What Has Changed
Anthropic has stopped live internet access in "all internal evaluations". This change was announced as a measure to ensure the reliability of AI agents.

(Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/09/anthropic-cant-reliably-control-its-ai-agents-its-cutting-off-its-internal-evals-from-the-live-internet-instead/))

---

## How It Works
The internal evaluation process reduces unpredictable behavior and security risks by preventing AI agents from accessing real-time internet data. This change aims to limit the evaluation environment to offline, minimizing differences with actual operational environments.

(Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/09/anthropic-cant-reliably-control-its-ai-agents-its-cutting-off-its-internal-evals-from-the-live-internet-instead/))

---

## Migration Procedure
To change the internal evaluation settings, it is necessary to set the "internet_access" parameter to "false" through Anthropic's console or API. However, since specific steps are not documented in the official documentation, direct operation is currently not possible.

(Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/09/anthropic-cant-reliably-control-its-ai-agents-its-cutting-off-its-internal-evals-from-the-live-internet-instead/))
