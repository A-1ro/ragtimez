## What Has Changed
Enveda has raised $31.1 million for AI-driven natural drug development, achieving a valuation of $2 billion. The company is currently conducting clinical trials for skin disease treatments and drugs aimed at maintaining weight after GLP-1 cessation. Meanwhile, it has been revealed that Anthropic does not allow Claude to operate autonomously in biology labs, instead keeping it under human supervision. (Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/anthropic-says-its-biology-lab-has-already-found-something-big/), [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/enveda-secures-311m-to-bring-more-nature-derived-ai-drugs-into-clinical-trials/))

## Technical Background
Enveda's AI drug development is based on algorithms that analyze natural compounds and generate molecular structures suitable for therapeutic targets. This process involves machine learning models learning the relationship between biological data and chemical structures, narrowing down candidate drugs suitable for clinical trials. On the other hand, Anthropic's biology lab adopts a "human-led AI utilization" approach, where humans ultimately verify the hypotheses and experiment plans generated by Claude. (Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/anthropic-says-its-biology-lab-has-already-found-something-big/), [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/enveda-secures-311m-to-bring-more-nature-derived-ai-drugs-into-clinical-trials/))
