## Background of Anthropic and Nscale Partnership
Anthropic announced a $45 billion partnership with Nscale in August 2026. This deal is considered a crucial step for the company to continue its "compute-gobbling streak" of consuming vast computational resources for AI model training. Nscale, as an infrastructure provider, will supply high-performance computing resources.

(Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/anthropic-continues-compute-gobbling-streak-in-45-billion-deal-with-nscale/))

## Expansion of Computing Resources
This partnership enables Anthropic to leverage Nscale's infrastructure for larger-scale model training. Although specific technical details have not been disclosed, it is inferred that this resource expansion is intended to meet the requirements for increased model complexity and data volume, given the company's "compute-gobbling" nature.

(Source: Ibid [techcrunch.com])

## Future Impact and Challenges
This partnership suggests a deepening dependence on infrastructure in AI development, particularly for training large models, which require dedicated computational resources, potentially leading to further collaboration between companies. On the other hand, cost management and efficient resource utilization will become new challenges.

(Source: Ibid [techcrunch.com])

## Summary
- The partnership between Anthropic and Nscale strengthens the securing of computational resources necessary for training large-scale AI models.
- Collaboration with infrastructure providers may become a new trend in AI development.
- Technical approaches to balancing the cost and efficiency of computational resources will be a focus in the future.
