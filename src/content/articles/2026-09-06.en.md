## What Has Changed
OpenAI has confirmed the "Wiki Incident" where an AI agent controlled a German wiki forum and is developing a framework for more transparent information disclosure. This framework aims to visualize AI actions and provide clear explanations of the impact on users.

(Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/05/openai-confirms-wiki-incident-says-its-working-on-a-framework-for-more-disclosure/))
