## Overview of Cognition's Acquisition of Poke

AI coding startup Cognition has acquired AI assistant "Poke". According to a report by TechCrunch, the acquisition was made at an evaluation of "low nine figures". Poke is positioned as an AI assistant that allows users to interact with it in a way that feels like sending a message to a friend.

The core argument of this acquisition is that the interaction model of AI assistants, which determines how they interact with users, is becoming just as important as the performance of the underlying models themselves. The title of the TechCrunch article, "AI personality is becoming a competitive advantage", also suggests that Cognition's acquisition decision symbolizes this trend.

(Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/why-cognition-bought-poke-ai-personality-is-becoming-a-competitive-advantage/))

## How Poke's Conversation Style Will Be Integrated into Devin

According to the TechCrunch article, the acquisition will bring Poke's "conversational style and interaction model" to Cognition's coding agent "Devin". This means that the acquisition is not just about acquiring technology assets or talent, but also about reflecting Poke's "way of talking to users" in Devin's experience.

However, the article does not provide details on how this integration will be implemented, such as the API design, data flow, or model architecture. It is unclear whether the conversation style will be transplanted at the prompt design level, or if it will involve a refresh of Devin's interface layer, or if it will be achieved through other technical means. The article does not include a link to official documentation.

(Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/why-cognition-bought-poke-ai-personality-is-becoming-a-competitive-advantage/))

## Background on AI Assistants' "Personality" Becoming a Competitive Axis

This acquisition suggests that in the coding agent market, the design of user experience, including "how to talk to users", is becoming a differentiating factor, in addition to model accuracy and speed. TechCrunch describes this as "a growing belief", and Cognition's management decision is cited as an example.

In this context, it is important to note that Poke was an assistant that could be used to "talk to friends in text". By incorporating this kind of conversational experience into Devin, a coding agent for developers, Cognition may be trying to differentiate the quality of interaction with developers. However, the detailed specifications of which functions or UI components will be reflected in Devin are not provided in the source.
